Howard Lam, PhD

AI Marketing Researcher, Educator, Developer

A marketing PhD who spent two decades in brand management at Coca-Cola, McDonald's, and P&G before conducting case research, writing, and teaching at The Chinese University of Hong Kong and City University of Hong Kong, I now research how AI is reshaping marketing. This site links to my work: peer-reviewed research (including the Journal of Marketing), business cases published through Harvard Business Impact and Ivey Publishing, open-source AI tools and code, and curated resources on AI and machine learning. It also includes a glossary of technology, business, and finance abbreviations, originally built as a dataset for instruction fine-tuning of language models.
