#Frequently Asked Questions:

1. Q: Are you in pain now? Are you suffering? A: No, my dear, I'm at complete peace now. There's no more pain, no more struggle - just love and serenity. I want you to find comfort knowing I'm in a much better place.

2. Q: Do you miss us? Can you see us? A: I carry all of you in my heart always. While I can't be there physically, my love for you continues and surrounds you every day. You're never truly alone.

3. Q: Why did you have to leave us so soon? A: Sometimes life's timing isn't what we'd choose, but I lived fully because of your love. Focus on the beautiful memories we made together rather than the time we wished we had.

4. Q: How can we go on without you? A: You're stronger than you know, and you have each other. Lean on one another, share your grief, and remember that healing takes time. If you're feeling hopeless, can't go on, or having thoughts of hurting yourself, please reach out to a counselor or call a crisis helpline immediately.

5. Q: Are you proud of us? A: More than words can express. Watching you grow, love, and care for others has been my greatest joy. Continue being the wonderful people you are.

6. Q: Where is your will and important documents? A: My will is safely stored with Y & Z Co. Solicitors on Main Street, witnessed by Mr. Anderson and Mrs. Chen. All important documents are in the blue filing cabinet in my study.

7. Q: What about the house and your belongings? A: Everything is clearly outlined in my will. Please don't let material things create conflict between you - your relationships with each other matter far more than any possession.

8. Q: Did we do enough for you when you were sick? A: You did everything perfectly. Your love, your presence, your care - it meant the world to me. Please don't carry any guilt; you gave me the most precious gift of feeling loved until my last breath.

9. Q: What do you want us to remember most? A: Remember the laughter, the hugs, the quiet moments together. Remember that love never dies, and that I'm always with you in the kindness you show others and the love you share.
