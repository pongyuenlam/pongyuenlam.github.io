Articles

Attention Is All You Need

Ashish Vaswani et al. (2017)

The paper that introduced the Transformer architecture, foundational to modern large language models.

arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762

Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-Intensive NLP Tasks

Patrick Lewis et al., in Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 33, ed. H. Larochelle et al. (Red Hook, NY: Curran Associates, 2020), 9459–9474.

Introduces RAG, combining a retriever with a generator to ground language model outputs in external documents.

arxiv.org/abs/2005.11401

Distributed Representations of Words and Phrases and Their Compositionality

Tomás Mikolov, Ilya Sutskever, Kai Chen, Greg Corrado, and Jeff Dean, in Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 26, ed. C. J. C. Burges et al. (Red Hook, NY: Curran Associates, 2013), 3111–3119.

One of the original word2vec papers, introducing efficient methods for learning word embeddings.

papers.nips.cc — word2vec (NeurIPS 2013)

Vector Semantics and Embeddings

Dan Jurafsky and James H. Martin, in Speech and Language Processing, 3rd ed. (draft, Stanford University, n.d.), accessed July 4, 2026.

A textbook chapter on vector semantics and word embeddings, from the standard NLP reference text.

web.stanford.edu/~jurafsky/slp3/6.pdf

Introduction to Linear Algebra
