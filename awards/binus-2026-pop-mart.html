3rd Place Winner, ASEAN Business Case Writing Competition 2026
Howard Pong-yuen Lam and Ervi Liusman (2026) · BINUS Center for Case Study · 24 September 2026

Certificate of Achievement

About the competition

The Business Case Writing Competition is run by the BINUS Business School Center for Case Study in Jakarta, Indonesia. Under “About BINUS Center for Case Study”, the page describes the Center as “advancing management education through the development, dissemination, and application of business cases.” (source) According to the competition’s Conditions, entries “must … be anonymous”, and under “How Your Case Will Be Evaluated”, “the Board of Judges will evaluate submissions.” (source)
